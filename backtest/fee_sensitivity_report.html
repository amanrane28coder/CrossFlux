CrossFlux · fee sensitivity · BTCUSDT vs XBT-USD · 2024-03-01

Raising fees cannot make this strategy lose money.

The backtest's entry gate is margin > fee. The PnL it books is margin − fee. Same inequality, same fee model — so a trade that could not cover its fee is never taken in the first place.

Charging more therefore does not produce losses. It produces fewer trades. Below, the round-trip fee goes from 0 to 14 basis points across four presets and the strategy stays profitable in every one, because the gate quietly raises its own bar to match.

The sieve

Each bar is mean gross edge per trade, split into the part the venues take and the part left over. Watch two things at once: the bars get longer as fees rise, and the trade population collapses.

fee taken, bps net kept, bps 1 dot = 10,000 filled trades

Why the bars grow

A wider fee forces the gate to hold out for a wider spread, so the trades that survive have more gross edge on average — 4.84 bps when fees are zero, 15.79 bps at retail rates. That rise is selection, not signal quality. The underlying predictor never changed between these four runs; only the threshold did.

Which means gross edge is not a property of the market being measured. It is a readout of the fee you fed in. Any figure quoted as "mean gross edge" is really just reporting the gate threshold back at you.

The identity, checked

If the gate and the PnL are the same inequality, then net edge must equal gross edge minus the fee in every run. It does, to within 0.005 bps — the small residual is real, not float error: each leg pays its own venue's rate on its own notional, and the two legs' notionals differ slightly. That near-exact identity is the arithmetic signature of a metric that cannot come out negative.
preset gross bps − fee bps = implied net measured net residual

What this does and doesn't show

Shown

The fee model is now single-sourced. One registry drives the Python backtest, the execution simulator and the C++ engine, and swapping presets needs no code edit. Rates agree across both languages to six decimal places.

Not shown

Any evidence the strategy has edge. Every return on this page is positive by construction. Until the entry criterion is independent of the PnL formula, no fee schedule can falsify it — and win rate is uninformative for the same reason, sitting between 96.9% and 99.6% regardless of preset.

Also not executable

The default preset implies 3.56 two-leg round trips per second sustained for 24 hours, with capital pre-positioned on both venues. Retail nets 1.78 bps per trade — thin enough that any unmodelled cost, from funding to rebalancing to partial fills, plausibly erases it.

Two corrections to earlier claims

  1. The institutional preset was described as 6 bps round-trip. It is 3.0 — Binance 1 bp plus Kraken 2 bps.
  2. Retail fees were expected to turn the strategy negative. They do not: retail returns +2.5%. The earlier negative figure came from an inconsistent setup, where the gate still used the old flat 5 bps per leg while the PnL used retail rates. Once both read one model, the sign returns to positive — which is the whole finding.
